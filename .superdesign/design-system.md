# Gemelo V2 design system

## Product
Gemelo (not Gememelo) lets testers try apps and leave reviews, and lets entrepreneurs run a paid campaign slot. Mobile-first phone column. French UI.

## Tokens
- Background `#071426`
- Cards `#10243a`
- Text `#f4f7fb`
- Muted `#aebed1`
- Accent `#d6ff4a` on dark, text on accent `#101d0a`
- Radius 18px, font Plus Jakarta Sans, body ≥ 16px
- Logo `/logo-gemelo.png` — never overflow its frame
- No global slogan under the logo
- No grey patches on pictos
- No PNG screenshot as a page background

## Settings (new page, no mockup)
Phone column. Back to profile. Title Réglages. Vertical list of full-width navy cards/buttons: CGV, CGU, Traitement des données, Contact. Chevron or arrow on the right. No login/logout row. No bottom navigation. Legal pages: Back + title + existing paragraphs. Contact: Back + title + Nom / E-mail / Message + lime Envoyer. Success: « Message enregistré sur cet appareil. »

## Constraints
- Offers 19 / 39 / 55 / 65 / 49 / 109 / 149 €
- Stripe simulated and labeled
- Real project names already in app: Bed Hop, Du rose au noir, Comptoir du Oui
- Do not invent social URLs or Chercher / Explorer / Message screens
